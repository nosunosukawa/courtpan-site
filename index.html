CourtPan Support

Automatic zoomed video and highlights from your basketball games, made on your Mac.

About CourtPan

CourtPan analyzes wide-angle basketball game videos (for example, from an iPhone on a stand) entirely on your Mac, and creates a zoomed video that follows the players and the ball, plus highlights.

  • Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later
  • Your videos never leave your Mac

Contact

For questions or bug reports, email nosunosukawa@gmail.com. For bugs, please include your Mac model, macOS version, and the length and resolution of the video.

FAQ

  • How long does processing take? It depends on your Mac and the length of the video. The processing screen shows an estimate of the time remaining.
  • Where are my exported videos? In the "CourtPan" folder inside your Movies folder.
  • Can I use my purchase on another Mac? Yes, on any Mac signed in with the same Apple Account, using "Restore Purchases" in the App.
