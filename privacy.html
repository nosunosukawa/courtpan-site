CourtPan Privacy Policy

CourtPan processes your videos only on your Mac. This policy explains how Nobutaka Kawasaki (Nosu Works, "we") handles information in the Mac app "CourtPan" (the "App").

Effective date: September 30, 2026
Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Summary

  • The App reads, analyzes, and exports your videos entirely on your Mac.
  • The App does not connect to the internet by itself. We do not send your videos or any analysis results to our servers or to anyone else. (Only when you make a purchase, restore purchases, or redeem a code does macOS's App Store system communicate with Apple; see section 3.)
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Because we do not track you, we treat everyone the same whether or not a browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal.
  • There is no account to create. We do not receive any personal information from you through the App.

Your videos may show people such as players, referees, and spectators. The App never sends them off your Mac. When you record games or share exported videos, please respect the people who appear in them (and their parents or guardians, for children) and follow the recording rules of the event and venue.

If you allow notifications, the App may notify you when processing is finished. Notifications are created on your Mac.

3. Purchases, restoring purchases, and offer codes

Purchases, restoring purchases, and redeeming offer codes are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, your Apple Account, or your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles this information.

Through App Store Connect, we receive aggregated sales and usage figures from Apple that do not identify you. If you have chosen to share crash reports or analytics with app developers in your Apple settings, we may receive those reports through Apple and use them only to fix problems in the App.

4. Contacting us, invitation codes, and this website (outside the App)

  • If you email us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to reply and to investigate problems.
  • If we give you an invitation code directly, we keep your name, contact details, and a record of the code we gave you, only to manage the codes (to avoid duplicates and check expiry).
  • We delete this information once its purpose is fulfilled, and no later than 2 years.
  • We use Gmail by Google LLC (United States) to receive and store email. The United States does not have a comprehensive federal privacy law equivalent to Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information; privacy is governed by sector-specific and state laws.
  • This page and the CourtPan support page are hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (United States). According to GitHub, the IP address of each visitor is logged and stored for security purposes. We have not added any analytics or advertising to these pages, and we do not use those logs.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where required by law. We never sell it or share it for advertising.

6. Security measures

  • Email is kept in an account used only by us.
  • Information inside the App stays on your Mac and is stored under macOS app protection (App Sandbox).

7. Your requests

To request access to, correction of, suspension of use of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you (section 4), email us at nosunosukawa@gmail.com. We will respond without undue delay after confirming your identity.

8. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If we learn that an email came from a child under 13, we will not keep it longer than needed to reply and will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with the "Last updated" date. If we make a significant change (for example, if the App starts sending information off your Mac), we will update this page before releasing that App update and will also notify you in the App.
